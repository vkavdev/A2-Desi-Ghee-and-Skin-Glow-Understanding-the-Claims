**Clarify What Someone Means by Glow**

Glow can mean quite different things to different people. Some use it for skin that feels less dry, others for a brighter complexion. It may also describe the surface sheen a rich ingredient leaves behind. These are distinct observations, not a single outcome.
That difference is important when evaluating A2 Desi ghee claims. Skin feeling softer after use does not on its own show changes in pigmentation, collagen or lasting skin health. A flattering photograph cannot establish the cause of an apparent difference either.
First, define the change you are seeking. Broad beauty descriptions can suggest that one simple ingredient answers every concern. Knowing your intended outcome helps you assess the wording and distinguish a passing appearance or subjective impression from evidence of a lasting effect.
